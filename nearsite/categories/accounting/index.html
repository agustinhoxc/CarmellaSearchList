What to ask before you hire

  • Ask whether the fee is monthly, hourly or per return, and what happens if the return needs an amendment.
  • Check which bookkeeping software the firm supports before you commit to one.
  • For a new company, confirm who is responsible for filing deadlines: you or the firm.

Questions about accounting

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Last year's return, current bank and card statements, and payroll records if you have employees. Firms that need anything else say so on their own page.

Can accounting be handled remotely?

Most of it can. The listings note whether a firm works entirely remotely, in person, or both.
